## Design

Use documented separate to/cc/bcc arrays instead of merging private recipients into the public to field. Preserve names, Reply-To handling and counts. Verify complete mocked payloads, including Cc-only and Bcc-only inputs and matching envelopes, on PHP 8.3/8.5 with PHPStan and formatting. No live provider delivery is needed.

Contract: [Scaleway email API](https://www.scaleway.com/en/developers/api/transactional-email/emails), checked on 2026-10-15 via Context7 and the official page. It documents dedicated to, cc and bcc address arrays. The required to field is still submitted for inputs without To addresses, as an empty array; provider acceptance of these edge-case payloads was not tested live.
